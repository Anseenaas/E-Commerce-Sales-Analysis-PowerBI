
# E-Commerce Sales Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end e-commerce sales analysis developed using **Power BI**. The project focuses on analyzing sales performance, profitability, regional performance, product performance, monthly sales trends, and customer payment preferences.

The dataset was prepared and transformed using **Power Query**, while **DAX measures** were used to calculate key business KPIs including Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Total Orders, and Average Order Value.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed using KPI cards, charts, slicers, and filters to transform the data into clear business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Monitor key business performance indicators.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Compare sales performance across regions.
- Identify profitable product categories.
- Identify high-performing sub-categories.
- Analyze customer payment preferences.
- Generate actionable business insights from sales data.

## Key Business Insights

- **North** is the highest-performing region, contributing approximately **27% of total sales**.
- **South** has the lowest regional sales contribution at approximately **23%**.
- **Furniture** generates the highest profit among the product categories.
- **Sofa** is the highest-performing sub-category by sales.
- Monthly sales remain relatively stable across much of the analyzed period, with **September 2025 showing the weakest sales performance**.
- No single payment method dominates sales, with payment methods contributing approximately **19%–21% each**.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate opportunities to improve sales performance in lower-performing regions while maintaining performance in stronger regions.
- Continue monitoring high-profit categories such as Furniture and identify opportunities to improve profitability across other categories.
- Use high-performing sub-categories such as Sofa to support inventory and promotional decisions.
- Investigate factors contributing to weaker sales periods and consider targeted promotions during lower-performing months.
- Continue supporting multiple payment options because customer payment preferences are relatively evenly distributed.
